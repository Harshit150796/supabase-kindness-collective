How we look after your gift and your information.

Encrypted connection

Every page, sign-in and payment on CouponDonation travels over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

Coupons, never cash

Every gift becomes store coupons or gift cards for the stores donors choose. Nothing is paid out as cash.

Payments by Square

You enter card details with Square, our payment processor. We never see or store full card numbers.
