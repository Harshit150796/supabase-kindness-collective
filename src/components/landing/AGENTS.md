# 3D tree rules
- Tree fruit animations share one transparent WebGL logo path.
- Tree slots stay stable while allowed brands rotate non-repeating; exclude Trader Joe's, eBay, Postmates, and Seamless.
- Use 18 deterministic, color-balanced canopy slots with front bias, orbit coverage, and no duplicate hanging brand.
- Use official local SVG artwork when available and preserve its colors/proportions; prefer recognizable emblems unless a brand is wordmark-led.
- Tree anchors use slot-specific leaf clearance and vertical correction.
- All 18 opening logos preload at tree-module evaluation (coupons painted ahead, one per task) and mount together; reveal follows a drawn full-logo frame, with a three-second safety timeout only outside the opening. Replacement logos preload on demand.
- Draco decoder is self-hosted at /draco/ (copied from three's bundled libs) so the tree never depends on a third-party host.
- FramePacer draws the tree itself (render priority 1): every frame until the reveal, then about 60fps on 120Hz-and-up screens so the GPU leaves the page room; PerfWatchdog counts drawn frames and steps quality down below 55fps on fine-pointer devices (45 on touch).
- The 3D tree canvas uses `frameloop='never'` while off-screen, tab-hidden, or until ShaderWarmup has compiled the scene (parallel via KHR_parallel_shader_compile, 5s deadline); demand mode starved page animations on phones.
- Production sets `checkShaderErrors` false (each check waits on the GPU). The environment map is built outside Suspense and the ground/leaf textures are drawn ahead, while the model downloads.
- Tree quality starts from shared desktop settings except known software renderers; committed visible-frame sampling reduces shadow/DPR costs before ambient density so screen size never selects artwork.
- Preserve context-owned MSAA and leaf alpha-to-coverage during adaptation; changing the antialias method would remount WebGL or alter the leaf silhouette.
- Tree model and appearance are shared across viewport sizes; camera aspect fitting and mobile label placement may vary, but lights, materials, exposure and geometry do not.
- Two skies only, chosen once per load: night 70%, blue day 30% (`html[data-sky]`, `?time3d=day|night` forces one); tapping the sky toggles them. No sunset/midday palette and no clock.
- There is no still tree picture and no part-built scenery (founder decision): the hero is plain page colour until the whole scene, tree and all, fades in together with the headline. Under the opening's cover it appears at once, because the opening holds until the tree has drawn. HeroBackdrop (src/lib/heroBackdrop.ts) paints the tree's own sky and ground only where the tree can't run (no WebGL, or an error), and announces ready.
- Nothing compiles or uploads inside a watched frame: the warm-up uploads textures in ~8ms slices alongside the parallel compile, a never-drawn group puts later effects (sprout, sparkle) into that first compile, and the 18 coupons stay hidden until compileAsync and their texture uploads finish.

## Homepage opening (also index.html and src/main.tsx)
- The homepage opening is the founder-approved logo-to-navbar lockup: off-white cover, logo mark, two-colour wordmark and "Transforming Giving" rise, one sheen, then the lockup flies onto the nav brand (`[data-nav-brand] img`, /brand/logo-256.webp) while the page settles in. It plays on every homepage load with no skip.
- It holds at least 1.55s and reveals when fonts, the nav brand and the drawn live tree are ready; a progress hairline appears after 1.8s; a 15s safety cap applies only if the tree never draws. Only transform/opacity animate. A homepage loaded in a background tab holds the lockup still (cd-held) and starts these timers only once the tab is shown.
- The inline script sets the sky before the homepage gate. The vite openingPreloads plugin writes the page and tree chunk lists into it at build time; tree chunks, model, Draco and logos preload only when WebGL works (window.__cdWebGL), which also gates main.tsx's early Tree3DScene import and lets the hero mount the tree at once. Its probe also records the GPU name (window.__cdGpu) for useDeviceTier, so startup makes no second throwaway WebGL context; any other probe releases its context immediately.
- Keep the index.html opening block under 12KiB. Canvas uses offsetSize, never scales.

## Homepage motion, reveals and sections
- Shared reveals decide via `revealMode` (pre-trigger 20% below viewport, late on-screen mounts still animate, time-based fling detection) so busy frames never skip motion.
- Editorial line reveals use removable aria-hidden overlays without replacing React-owned nodes; font/initial-frame settlement and the shared observer protect startup. Headings split only after their own faces load (italic runs included), and the painted lines stack in normal flow so they never drift off the real ones.
- Cursor uses a settling rAF loop timed by the rAF timestamp, falling back to the clock past 6ms (frameDt); preserve elapsed time through delayed frames, reset at one-second interruptions and hide/reset on tab visibility loss so the trail never replays stale movement. Capture pointer targets before canvas interaction work, cache DOM colours, and avoid unchanged/hidden native-lead transform writes; CSS owns colour/hover. Over the live tree and its sky the cursor is fixed blue (founder-chosen) and, on the tree canvas, the lead dot is the system pointer (a blue-dot image set, OS-drawn so it never waits on the tree); never read pixels back from WebGL (each read stalls on the GPU).
- Lazy section short states fill reservations to preserve scroll; shared count/retry logic prevents false zeros and early failures.
- Homepage section code prefetches one module per idle period after the opening (src/lib/idlePrefetch.ts, skipped on Save-Data); sections still mount on view. Scroll handlers never read layout per frame (use IntersectionObserver).
- Homepage "Give what people need." steps: laptops (lg+) deal four alternating logo-green/blue cards via transform-only element.animate with px values. Dedicated actual-visibility gating and timing live in src/lib/cardDealTiming.ts, independent of early heading reveals, so the sequence is seen rather than spent before arrival; unfinished deals pause off-screen/tab-hidden and resume, re-stacking only below the viewport. Phones keep the compact animated list unchanged.
- Testimonials are "Voices in motion" (founder-chosen): two rows of lines lifted word for word from each published account (testimonialLines.ts: whole sentences, or a clause or ending marked with an ellipsis, never reworded) drift continuously in opposite directions as compositor-only CSS animations; choosing a line shows the full account in a panel sized to the longest one while automatic rotation continues, so nothing below shifts.

## Donate card (DonationFlow + donate/)
- Three steps (Retailers, Amount, Review); from the chosen amount on, a deep-green "Your gift" ledger: the amount, a split bar and each retailer's dollars; on phones the ledger is a strip above the steps that lists retailers only on Review.
- The split is dollars, never percentages: "Evenly" or "I'll choose" (steppers and typed amounts via setShare, so it always adds up). Presets below $10 x retailers are disabled; changing retailers resets to even.
- No suggested amount: step 1 is full width with no gift panel; the panel slides open beside (phones: above) the steps once the donor picks an amount on step 2.
- No coupon counts, no coupon sizes, no fake payment icons: Review explains that the team sizes each store's coupons, and shows real Visa/Mastercard/Amex marks.


## "Every dollar" section (TrustTransparency)
- The Every dollar band uses scoped semantic surface/ink tokens and breakpoint-sized serif typography, so its contrasting treatment stays readable without changing other sections. Preserve the promise headline, seven-second underline, scroll-filled/draining 100%, three promises and journey; never show 95%/platform-fee splits. Recurring underline work stops off-screen/tab-hidden; journey geometry is cached on resize/layout changes without layout or SVG geometry reads in scroll frames. Journey timing (journeyScrollTiming.ts) selects discrete icon anchors: one stop per wheel gesture, grouped across momentum events, with reversible scroll checkpoints for other inputs and no scroll trapping. A fractional-stop transform follows cached line segments during travel and retargets from its current position; frames stop on arrival or invisibility, so the coupon travels visibly but rests only on icons.
- The hero headline is the founder-chosen "two halves meet" (HeroHeadline.tsx, CSS under "Hero headline and actions"): "CouponDonation is" plus a serif word sliding in every 3s (transparent green, trackable blue, real-time light at night; deep versions by day), and one capsule split into Donate now (logo green, left) and Apply as Recipient (logo blue, right) with a heart in the 4px seam. The halves slide in and meet once the scene shows and the opening has lifted (`data-play`), the heart pops in, then every 9s the halves breathe apart and back while the heart beats. They never hide or fade out once shown. The old TVA glow buttons are gone. It is always centred and rests on the canopy (founder decision): HeroHeadline writes the canopy top from the tree camera (canopyTopPx in src/lib/heroBackdrop.ts) to --canopy and the buttons end 12px above it. Sizes are fixed and small (buttons 36px phones / 38px wider, 13-13.5px text; kicker 12.5-13px with an 18-20px serif word), never scaled with the window, and no extra lines. On laptop framing the moon and sun move right just enough to clear the buttons (orbX, ORB_CLEARANCE_PX; founder-approved exception to the no-scene-change rule); phones keep them where they were.
